Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy covers "Agent", a personal assistant application operated by Dave Elfving for his own use. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's explicit consent through Google's sign-in screen, Agent may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data belonging to that account.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks requested by the account owner, such as reading and sending email, managing calendar events and working with documents. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and any retrieved data are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
